Engineering principles

How I think about modern quality engineering

Q

Quality as Engineering

Testing is a core engineering discipline, not a final validation phase.

A

Automation as a Platform

Enterprise automation should be reusable, observable, governed and scalable.

E

AI Requires Evaluation

LLMs and agents need systematic checks across accuracy, reliability, safety, cost and performance.

H

Human + AI Collaboration

AI should accelerate engineering while human oversight remains explicit for high-impact decisions.

D

Evidence-Driven Quality

Logs, metrics, traces, screenshots and reports should make quality observable.
